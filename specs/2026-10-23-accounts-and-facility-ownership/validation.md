## Phase 8a: moves and closures (FR-53, US-20; draft PR, 2026-10-27)

**What changed:** `POST /listings/{id}/move` (editor, admin, or a provider with an approved link) writes one audit row per changed field with the old and the new value, puts a **published** listing to `recheck_due` (it leaves search, `/emergency` and its page at once and returns only after staff re-check and publish it), sets `next_check_due` to now, and resets the pin to the default centre when the **owner** moves it (staff set the real pin). `POST /listings/{id}/close` (editor, admin, or an approved **owner**; not a manager) hides the listing and sets `closed_at` and `closed_reason`; phones, hours, services, checks and the audit log stay. `GET /listings/{id}/address-history` lists address, area and landmark changes newest first, including the older plain edits, without staff names for a provider. `reopen` clears the closure. A provider's move or closing emails the editors and admins (name only). One test proves no route in the API uses `DELETE`. **Screens:** one component, "Moved to a new address? / Closed for good? / Address history", under the staff editor (editor and admin) and on "Edit my facility"; both ask before acting; the report queue shows **Start a move** on a "moved" report and **Close it** on a "closed" one.
- **Migration** `b8a1c3d5e7f9` (after `7d1c4b9e5a32`): `listings.closed_at` and `listings.closed_reason`, both nullable; no row is rewritten, no status value or table is added; the downgrade drops only the two columns. Tested from "the database as it was" (5 statuses): columns appear null, rows unchanged, downgrade, up-down-up, the status enum still has its 5 values and the table count is 10.
- **Backend (tests first):** `test_listing_address_moves.py` (58: access for every role and link state, rows written, second move, nothing changed, 422s, status rules, the pin, alerts, closing, closed listing hidden everywhere, reopen, nightly job, history, no DELETE route) and `test_listing_closure_migration.py` (5). The status-move file `test_listing_moves.py` is unchanged.
- **Frontend (tests first):** `MoveOrClose.test.tsx` (18), cases in `ListingEditor`, `ProviderScreens` and `ReportsQueue` tests.
- **Render / Netlify:** **a migration runs on deploy** (revision `b8a1c3d5e7f9`: two empty nullable columns on `listings`). Nothing is dropped or changed. Confirm the latest backup before merging (NFR-09). No variable, no package.

## Phase 7: register or claim a facility (FR-50, FR-51, US-17; draft PR, 2026-10-27)

**What changed:** the open "List your facility" form is gone. `POST /facility-submissions` always answers 401 ("Sign up or log in to list your facility.") and writes nothing; drafts sent before stay as they were. A provider uses **`POST /provider/facilities`** (a draft as before, a `pending` owner link with the chosen role, audit rows `create` and `owner_request` carrying the provider's id; the contact details are the account's own) or **`GET /provider/claimable?q=` + `POST /provider/claims`** (find a published or re-check-due facility by name or area and ask to manage it; a reason of 3 to 300 characters). **Duplicate check (D15):** the same phone number on a listing of any status, or a name with trigram similarity 0.6 or more in the same area, stops the registration with 409; a published or re-check-due listing is named by id so the person can claim it, a draft or hidden one only gets "This facility is already registered. Please contact us." Nothing is written on a 409. Rate limits: 3 registrations and 10 claims per address per day, 60 searches per hour. The claim search returns only id, name, area and category (no phone, address or description, and never a draft or hidden listing). The staff queue shows "Their reason". **Page:** a visitor sees why an account is needed and **Sign up** (only while sign-up is open) and **Log in** (which brings a provider back here); a provider sees "Register a new facility" and "Find and claim your facility"; a staff account is sent to the admin screens. The login accepts `?next=/list-your-facility` for a provider and nothing else.
- **Backend (tests first):** `test_register_and_claim.py` (54 cases: access, registration, duplicates, claim search, claim, limits, queue), `test_facility_submissions.py` rewritten (7), `test_swiftrescue_groups_categories` now uses the new route, `test_owner_links` knows the `reason` field.
- **Frontend (tests first):** `ListYourFacility.test.tsx` (37: visitor, staff, register, claim, return address, no storage), a `LoginForm` case, an `OwnerRequests` case for the reason. The old `FacilityForm` and its test are replaced. **Real browser** (`facility.spec.ts`, 20 tests at 320, 360, 768 and 1024 px, with a faked API): the visitor, the registration, a wrong phone, the 409 to claim road, the 429. Not run: the whole road with a real database (sign-up, confirm, register, staff approve, provider edits); the pieces are each covered, and it is on the owner's list for the preview.
- **Render / Netlify:** nothing to change, no migration, no new variable.
- **Not here:** moves, closures and branches (Phase 8); Privacy and Terms text (Phase 9).

## Phase 5: account self-service (FR-54, FR-56, US-21; draft PR, 2026-10-27)

**What a person can do now:** `/account` (link **Account** in the staff bar, every role): change the name; change the email (a code goes to the NEW address, the old one works until it is confirmed, the old address gets a notice); change the phone (with Termii live a code is texted to the new number; without Termii it is stored at once, not confirmed, and an admin phones to confirm it, D20); change the password (current one needed; every older login stops working and the screen keeps the person logged in with a fresh token); close the account (password and the word CLOSE, then a question; name, email, phone and note are removed, the row and the audit log stay; refused for the last active admin). `/forgot-password` (link "Forgot your password?" under the login's password): the email, then a 6-digit code and the new password; the answer is the same for every email; it does not log the person in. `/reset-password` redirects there.
- **Backend (tests first, 62):** `test_account_self_service.py` covers each route with a wrong, an expired, a foreign and a right code; the old email works until the new one is confirmed; an email another account has is refused at both steps; the password rule per role; a wrong current password and its limit (10 in 15 minutes); the older token refused after a change and the fresh one accepted; forgot-password identical for a known and an unknown email and for an account that cannot log in; a reset code works once; close keeps the audit rows and removes every personal column; the last admin cannot close; nothing secret in a log, an audit entry or an answer. New in the core: access tokens carry their issue time in milliseconds (`ims`), and `get_user_for_token` refuses one made before `password_changed_at` (decision D10); code tokens can carry the new email or phone as a signed claim. **No migration** (the columns exist since Phase 2).
- **Frontend (tests first):** `AccountSettings.test.tsx` (38), `ForgotPassword.test.tsx`, additions to the staff bar tests; every password input is the shared `PasswordField`; the fresh token replaces the old in the tab's session and no password is stored; `StaffGate` gained `replaceToken`.
- **Render / Netlify:** nothing to set, no migration, no package. Sign-up stays closed. Email codes need Resend as before (without it the pages say "Email is not set up yet." in production, and log the code in development).
- **Known limits:** the timing of forgot-password differs a little for a known email (api-spec 4d); the owner links are marked removed on closing only from Phase 6, when that table exists.
